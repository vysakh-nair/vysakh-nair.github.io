About

Hello, I'm Vysakh. Designer. Road-tripper. Ramen experimenter.

Founding product designer at Senpi. I went to college for computer science, discovered in my second year that creativity could be an actual job, and never quite recovered.

The work, briefly

The products changed. The problems got harder. My role grew with them.

I started in visual design, moved into UX, and became a founding product designer working directly with founders and engineering teams — through one company's evolution from blockchain infrastructure to creator economy to autonomous AI trading. I stay involved until something useful ships. The full story is the walk on the homepage; the compressed version is the résumé.
