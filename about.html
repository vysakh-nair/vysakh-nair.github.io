About

Hello, I'm Vysakh.

Founding product designer at Senpi — road-tripper and ramen experimenter on weekends. I went to college for computer science, discovered creativity could be an actual job, and never quite recovered.

The work, briefly

The products changed. The problems got harder. My role grew with them.

I gravitate toward problems where the interface is only the visible part of the challenge. Over the last five years that has meant developer infrastructure, financial products, crypto and autonomous AI — mostly in early-stage teams where the product itself was still being figured out, working closely with founders and engineers. The full story is the walk on the homepage.

“Imagine a world without design — everything would function, but nothing would connect.”
