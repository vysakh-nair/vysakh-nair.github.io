About

Hello, I'm Vysakh.

Founding product designer at Senpi. I studied computer science, found out design could be a job, and haven't looked back. Weekends are for road trips and ramen experiments.

The work, briefly

The products changed. The problems got harder. My role grew with them.

I'm drawn to problems where the interface is only the visible part. For five years that has meant developer infrastructure, financial products, crypto and autonomous AI, mostly in early-stage teams still shaping the product, working closely with founders and engineers. The full story is the walk on the homepage.
